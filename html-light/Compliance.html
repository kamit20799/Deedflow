RERA · Section 13

No receipt crosses 10% before the agreement is registered.

Section 13 of RERA limits what a promoter can take as advance or application fee to 10% of the cost until a written agreement for sale is registered. DeedFlow checks this at the moment a receipt is entered, not at month-end.

TDS · 194-IA & 194H

The TDS on both sides of a sale, worked out for you.

On the buyer side, 194-IA applies when a property's consideration is ₹50 lakh or more. On the partner side, 194H applies to brokerage and commission. DeedFlow tracks both so receipts reconcile and payout runs deduct correctly.
