About

Built for the way Indian developers sell.

DeedFlow is an independent, multi-tenant SaaS CRM for property developers. We started from the money flow, because that is where generic CRMs break for Indian real estate: RERA caps, escrow splits, GST, TDS on both sides of a sale, and auditors who want to see every change.

So the landing page only shows what ships. If a feature is on this site, it is in the product today.

01Only what shipsThe site lists capabilities that are in the product today, nothing on a roadmap.
02Compliance in the flowRules run when money moves, so reports are a by-product, not a project.
03One record per buyerDedupe at capture and a single ledger keep every team on the same facts.
04Every change on the recordA strict audit trail across leads, pricing, receipts and payouts.
